# Archived annual letter draft

Preserved as source material when the sponsor report shifted to per-event editions.

## Draft opening letter · based on nifty’s reflections

To our partners at NYDIG, HRF, and Btrust,

Three things stand out when I think about what I’m proudest of at bitcoin++ this year: going to Africa, shipping our hackathon platform, and the conversations we helped spark at events around the globe.

Bringing bitcoin++ to Africa was a personal highlight. As we expand, I’m excited about the opportunity to bring more people into the conversation about Bitcoin’s technical future—and to hear what matters to them.

We also shipped our own hackathon platform, with help from Jake, our summer intern from Tulsa. I’m proud of that work and grateful for his contribution. Building tools to support the people who come to our events is an investment in what bitcoin++ can become.

Our first-ever economics edition was another highlight. You sometimes hear that “developers don’t understand economics.” I was excited to help bridge that gap: to bring developers and people making that argument into a conversation with one another. Bitcoin needs places where people can ask difficult questions across disciplines, explain their assumptions, and listen.

Those conversations are part of what makes this work meaningful to me. Alongside the attendance figures and hackathon projects in this report, I want to share the pride I feel in helping make them happen.

Thank you for supporting bitcoin++ and the community gathering around it.

nifty

The Africa visit, platform launch, Jake’s contribution, and economics edition are based on nifty’s notes. The wording remains a draft for her review. Lessons and frustrations from expansion have not yet been supplied and remain open prompts.
