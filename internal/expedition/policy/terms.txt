Your passage

The expedition runs January 24–February 5, 2028. Fares are per person in USD. Your group selects cabin types and the number of berths; specific cabin numbers and deck locations are not guaranteed. We will try to match M, F or co-ed roommate preferences in shared twin cabins. Triple and quad cabins default to co-ed.

Payment options

Choose full payment at signup or equal installments across signup and the remaining scheduled dates: January 1, April 1, July 1 and October 1, 2027. Dates on or before the signup date (America/Chicago) are omitted. Any rounding difference is included in the final payment. Your payment schedule is fixed when you book. If no scheduled dates remain, payment in full is required. The non-refundable portion remains 20% of the passage fare even if the signup payment is larger. Full payment in bitcoin receives a 5% passage discount. Payment is available in bitcoin or by card via Stripe. Installments use the standard fare. Merchandise is separate, paid upfront, and collected onboard.

Operator deposit and payment deadline

The operator requires a 20% deposit per traveler as they book and describes that deposit as non-refundable. The operator requires final payment 70 days before departure: November 15, 2027, assuming the January 24 departure date. Our installment plan finishes earlier, on October 1, 2027. Our guest cancellation policy appears below.

Cabin availability and confirmation

Our cabin allocation is currently held on a right-of-first-refusal basis. If another passenger requests a held cabin, the operator can require us to confirm it with a deposit or release it within 24–48 hours. This is an operator hold, not a confirmed passenger booking. Contact inbox@btcpp.dev for assistance with booking confirmation or availability.

Insurance you must arrange

Travel insurance is required and is not included in your fare. It must cover medical treatment, accidents, evacuation and repatriation in Antarctica. The operator currently requires at least EUR/USD 100,000 per person for medical evacuation and repatriation. Ask your insurer to confirm coverage for this destination and your planned activities. Cancellation and interruption cover are strongly recommended; they are separate from the mandatory medical and evacuation cover. We will confirm proof-of-insurance requirements before travel.

Costs to plan for separately

Flights, pre- and post-cruise stays, meals ashore, passports and visas, government arrival/departure taxes, insurance, gratuities and personal purchases are excluded. The cruise does not supply parkas. The bitcoin++ parka is a separate organizer arrangement; contact us about inclusion and delivery. Cruise port charges do not settle the tax treatment of our own booking.

Activities and itinerary changes

Landings, wildlife sightings and individual activities are not guaranteed. Weather, ice, permits and environmental restrictions can change the route and program. The expedition leader decides the final plan. Activities depend on safe conditions, capacity, appropriate equipment and participant suitability. Follow the crew’s safety and environmental instructions. Activity registration and equipment requirements will be confirmed in your departure information.

Traveler information and documents

Each traveler must provide accurate personal information, complete required operator forms and carry the necessary passport, visas and other travel documents. Tell the operator about health or mobility needs relevant to safe participation through its designated process. Oceanwide requires a Personal Information Form (PIF), including personal, passport, emergency-contact and medical information. Its published guidance says to return the PIF within one month of booking. Boot sizing and activity forms are included where applicable. Insurance policy details are also requested after booking. We will provide the operator’s current form or personal completion link and confirm any departure-specific instructions or additional medical clearance requirements. A group purchaser’s booking acceptance does not replace each traveler’s required forms.

Guest payment, cancellation and substitutions

Final guest payment is due by the end of October 1, 2027, in America/Chicago time. Refund eligibility ends at the start of that date.

Cancellation requests received before 12:00 a.m. on October 1, 2027, in America/Chicago time are eligible for a refund of passage payments received, less the initial 20% deposit, which is non-refundable. No refund is available for guest cancellations received at or after that cutoff. Send cancellation requests to inbox@btcpp.dev. Passenger substitutions are not permitted.

Bitcoin refund valuation

Bitcoin payments are valued in USD at their value on the day each payment was received. Refund entitlement is calculated from those recorded USD amounts, less the non-refundable deposit. A refund paid in Bitcoin is converted from the refundable USD amount at the exchange rate when the refund is sent; the number of satoshis returned may differ from the number originally paid.

Missed installments and abandonment

Installments are due by the end of their stated date in Texas time (America/Chicago). We send reminders 14 days before, 4 days before, on the due date, and every other day overdue through day 28. If an installment remains unpaid for 30 days after its deadline, the reservation is marked abandoned, its berths released, and all payments already made forfeited. We notify the purchaser and our expedition team when forfeiture is completed.

Abandonment for non-payment is distinct from a guest-requested cancellation. An unpaid checkout hold simply expires; it is not a confirmed booking and does not trigger forfeiture. Payment processing or reconciliation issues require review before abandonment. Your accepted booking terms are retained with your purchase.
